# Spider-Man: Brand New Day

## Overview
*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* is the fourth standalone Spider-Man film starring Tom Holland as Peter Parker.
The movie continues Peter's story after the events of *Spider-Man: No Way Home*.
It is set about four years after Doctor Strange's spell erased Peter Parker from everyone's memories.
Peter now lives alone in New York City, and the world no longer remembers his personal identity.
The film explores what it means for Peter to become Spider-Man full-time.

## Story
Peter has sacrificed his relationships with his closest friends to protect them.
Although MJ and Ned have moved forward with their lives, Peter still remembers their shared history.
He focuses on fighting crime while dealing with loneliness and the emotional cost of his decision.
The pressure of living without a support network begins to affect him.
His spider-senses also undergo a mysterious change.
A new threat emerges in New York, connected to a powerful villain who is difficult to perceive.
Peter must investigate the danger while trying to protect the people he cares about.
The story emphasizes responsibility, resilience, and the importance of community.
It also examines whether being a hero means facing every problem alone.

## Characters and Cast
Tom Holland returns as Peter Parker, also known as Spider-Man.
Zendaya plays Michelle “MJ” Jones-Watson, who no longer remembers Peter.
Jacob Batalon reprises his role as Ned Leeds, Peter's former best friend.
Jon Bernthal appears as Frank Castle, also known as the Punisher.
Mark Ruffalo returns as Bruce Banner, whose scientific knowledge may help Peter.
Sadie Sink portrays Jean Grey, a young character with extraordinary psychic abilities.
Michael Mando returns as Mac Gargan, also known as Scorpion.
Tramell Tillman is also part of the film's cast.

## Production
Destin Daniel Cretton directs the film.
The screenplay is written by Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers.
The story is based on Marvel comic-book characters created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko.
The film is produced by Kevin Feige, Amy Pascal, Avi Arad, and Rachel O'Connor.
Sony Pictures and Marvel Studios are associated with the production.
The movie blends superhero action with a more personal, street-level story.
Its tone is described as grittier and more emotional while retaining humor.
The film was released in theaters in July 2026.

## Themes and Significance
Peter's isolation is a central emotional conflict.
The film highlights the value of friendship, trust, and asking others for help.
It continues the Marvel Cinematic Universe story following *No Way Home*.
The Punisher's presence introduces a contrast between Peter's restraint and Frank Castle's harsher methods.
The movie presents a new chapter in Peter Parker's life as he balances his identity and his responsibility as Spider-Man.
